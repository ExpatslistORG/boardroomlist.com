# BoardroomList

[BoardroomList](https://boardroomlist.com) is a job search platform for Manager-to-CEO positions at large US corporations. Instead of aggregating listings already flooded with applicants on LinkedIn or Indeed, it pulls roles directly from Fortune 500 company career pages, so a candidate applies straight to the hiring team rather than into an automated screening queue.

## Searching roles

Role search at [/roles#search](https://boardroomlist.com/roles#search) covers tens of thousands of manager-to-CEO positions sourced directly from Fortune 500 corporate sites. Each individual listing lives at its own URL under [/roles](https://boardroomlist.com/roles), showing the role, company, and salary range where the employer discloses one.

## Why apply directly

Mass job boards mean mass competition and automated rejection systems that filter out qualified candidates before a human ever sees a resume. Applying directly to the hiring team through a sourced link means less competition, fewer applicants per role, and no auto-rejection filter standing between a candidate and the hiring manager.

## Salary transparency

Where a Fortune 500 employer publishes a salary range for a role, it shows directly on the listing, so a candidate can filter for compensation before applying rather than after an interview.

## Developer and AI agent access

A developer API and MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration, documented at [/developers](https://boardroomlist.com/developers), lets an AI job-search agent query executive roles programmatically on behalf of a candidate.
